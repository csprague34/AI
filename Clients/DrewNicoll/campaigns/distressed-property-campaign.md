## Overview

Major pivot from Mendocino + Sonoma County to a nationwide distressed property lead generation campaign. Drew targets sellers across all 50 approved states who are unable or unwilling to sell through traditional channels.

### Target Sellers

- Cannot sell due to repairs, code violations, tax liens, probate, title issues, or judgments
- Behind on mortgage or facing foreclosure
- Inherited unwanted property
- Need creative financing solutions — subject-to, wrap mortgage, owner carry
