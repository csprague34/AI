**Runs:** Every weekday at 9:00 AM
**Goal:** Contact 10 ideal targets per day to build Sprague AI client pipeline
**Status:** ACTIVE

Send to local service businesses that need leads but aren't running real digital marketing yet.

|----------|-------------|-------------------|

## EMAIL TEMPLATE

**Subject:** Quick question about your leads

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I run Sprague AI — we build done-for-you lead generation systems for local businesses in [their area]. I noticed [one specific thing about their business — e.g. "you're running ads but no landing page" or "your Google reviews are strong but I don't see a lead capture anywhere"].
>
> We handle the whole thing — ads, landing page, lead notifications, follow-up — and you only pay when the system is set up and running. Would it make sense to talk this week?
>
> — Christopher Sprague | Sprague AI | spragueai.com

**Rules:**
- Always personalize the middle sentence with ONE specific observation
- Never use "I hope this email finds you well" or any filler opener
- Never attach anything — keep it text only
- Always end with a question, never a statement

---

## DAILY EXECUTION STEPS

1. Pull 10 new targets from Google Maps or LinkedIn (use search terms below)
2. Find first name + email for each (LinkedIn, website contact page, or Hunter.io)
3. Write the personalized middle sentence for each — visit their website first
4. Send all 10 emails from Christopher's outreach email account
5. Log every send to `Agency/outreach-log.md` with date, name, business, email, personalization used
6. Flag any replies to Christopher immediately via WhatsApp:
   > "Reply from [Name] at [Business] — [paste first line of their reply]"
7. If reply is positive → draft meeting request for Christopher to send within 1 hour
